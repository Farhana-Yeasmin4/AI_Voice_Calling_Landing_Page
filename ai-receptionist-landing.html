How it works

We set it up. You get the leads.

No apps to install and nothing to configure yourself. Oleyn builds, tests and looks after Shrobon AI Calling for you.

  1. Tell us about your business

    Your hours, service areas, lead form questions and the answers the AI is allowed to give.

  2. We set up your number

    You get a local AI number, and we set up forwarding from your existing line, including business numbers routed by your provider.

  3. We ring-test it with you

    We test every forwarding mode and make test calls before your receptionist goes live.

  4. We review and improve it

    We review calls daily in the first week, then weekly. Every change is tested before your callers hear it.

01 · On the call

Polite, quick and on script

The AI handles the conversation the way you would, and keeps calls short.

Asks your questions

The AI follows your own lead form: name, location, service, details, email and timing. Optional questions are only asked if the caller is happy to keep going.

Knows who's calling

It tells new enquiries apart from existing customers, suppliers and sales calls. Only new enquiries get your lead questions; everyone else just gives a name and reason.

Flags urgent calls

Words like "leaking", "damage" or "today" mark a call as urgent in the email, Sheet and dashboard, so you return it first.

Urgent

Reads details back

It spells emails back and reads back any different call-back number. It never asks for a number caller ID already shows.

Natural conversation

Replies in about 0.8 seconds, and callers can interrupt at any time, just like talking to a person.

Answers only what you approve

If there's no approved answer, the AI tells the caller you'll follow up and passes the question to you. Approve an answer and it can handle it next time.

02 · After the call

Every enquiry, ready to act on

Details land where you already work, with a clear note on anything worth double-checking.

Email within a minute

Writes to your Google Sheet

One row per call, matched to your column names, with phone numbers keeping their leading 0. Your own Status and Notes columns are never touched.

Recordings & transcripts

Both sides of every call are recorded. Play it back, or tap a line in the transcript to jump to that moment.

Tells you what to check

If the AI isn't sure of a detail, like an email heard somewhere noisy, it marks it "Check". Fix it in one click.

How minutes work

Clear rules, no surprises

You can see your usage in the dashboard at any time.

  1. Rounded up per call

    Each call is rounded up to the next minute. A 3:12 call counts as 4 minutes.

  2. Short calls are free

    Calls under 10 seconds, like hang-ups and wrong numbers, aren't counted.

  3. Alerts before you run out

    We email you at 80% and again at 100% of your minutes.

  4. Reset every month

    Your minutes reset on the 1st. If you reach your limit, calls go to your own number or recorded message, so none are lost.
